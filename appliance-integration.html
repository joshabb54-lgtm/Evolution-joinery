Home/Services/Appliance Integration

Integrated appliances make a kitchen look clean and considered. When they are fitted well, the oven sits flush with the cabinetry, the dishwasher door matches the surrounding units, and the fridge-freezer disappears behind its panel. When they are fitted badly, you have doors that do not align, appliances that rattle when opened, and gaps that collect crumbs and grease.

Getting appliance integration right is a detail-focused job. It requires precise cabinet preparation, careful fitting of door hinges and anti-tilt mechanisms, and patience to get everything aligned before it is signed off. We do this as part of our full kitchen installation service and also as standalone work for single appliances or replacement units.

Appliances we fit and integrate

  • Built-in ovens and combination microwaves — Single and double ovens fitted into housing units, secured correctly, and with trim kits applied where required.
  • Hobs — Gas, induction, and ceramic hobs cut into worktops and fitted flush. We work closely with the worktop fitting stage to ensure hob cutouts are precise.
  • Integrated dishwashers — Door panels fitted and aligned with surrounding unit doors. Anti-tilt mechanism and hinges adjusted so the door opens and closes cleanly.
  • Integrated fridge-freezers — Full-height and under-counter integrated fridges and fridge-freezers fitted with correct door panels. Ventilation and fixing requirements followed precisely.
  • Extractor fans and cooker hoods — Canopy extractors and chimney hoods fitted and secured to the correct height and position above the hob.
  • Washing machines and dishwashers in utility rooms — Under-counter appliance fitting for utility rooms, including any required worktop cutouts.

Why appliance integration needs care

Many homeowners underestimate how fiddly integrated appliances are to fit correctly. The cabinet housing needs to be the right internal size for the appliance. The door panel needs to be cut to exactly the right dimensions and attached with the correct fixing kit for that appliance model. The hinge mechanism needs to be adjusted so the door swings in line with surrounding units.

When this is rushed or done without the right knowledge, problems appear quickly — doors that hang at an angle, panels that do not match the surrounding cabinetry, or appliances that are not properly secured in their housing. We follow manufacturer guidance precisely and check alignment carefully before finishing.

What good appliance integration looks like
  • Doors sit flush with surrounding cabinet doors
  • Gap between integrated door and adjacent units is consistent
  • Appliance is fully secure in its housing with no movement
  • Door opens and closes without catching or dragging
  • Trim strips and kick plates are fitted neatly
  • Hob sits flush to the worktop with a clean seal

Frequently asked questions

Can you fit a new appliance into an existing kitchen?
Yes — we carry out single appliance integration jobs as well as full kitchen installations. If you are replacing a dishwasher, oven, or fridge-freezer in an existing kitchen, we can fit and integrate the new unit.
Do you work with all appliance brands?
Yes. We work with all major brands including Bosch, Siemens, Neff, AEG, Miele, Hotpoint, Beko, and others. Each has specific fitting requirements and we follow the manufacturer instructions for each.
Do you connect gas appliances?
Gas connection must be carried out by a Gas Safe registered engineer. We can coordinate this alongside the fitting if needed, but the gas connection itself is not part of our service.
Do you connect electrical appliances?
We handle the physical fitting and integration of electrical appliances. Hard-wired connections (such as electric hobs and electric ovens) require a qualified electrician, which we can coordinate with.
Can you fit a hob into an existing worktop?
Yes, providing the worktop material allows for it. We can cut the aperture for a new hob into an existing laminate, wood, or quartz worktop and fit the hob flush. See our worktop fitting page for more detail.
