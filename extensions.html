Extensions in York. Built right.

High-quality joinery and internal fit-out for extensions across York. We work alongside builders and architects to deliver extensions that are finished to a standard you can see.

Home/Services/Extensions

An extension adds space and value to your home — but only if it is built and finished well. The structural work might be the most visible part of the process, but it is the internal joinery and finish that determines how the space feels to live in day to day.

We handle the joinery and internal carpentry for extensions across York and the surrounding area. We work alongside builders, architects, and project managers on full extension projects, or we can take on the joinery package for a structurally complete extension.

What our extension joinery service covers

  • First and second fix carpentry — structural timber work through to the finished joinery on new extension spaces
  • Door and window frames — internal lining, reveals, and finishing around new openings
  • Fitted kitchens — kitchen fitting in open-plan extensions is one of the most common requests. See our kitchen installation page for detail.
  • Staircases — new stairs for two-storey extensions. See our staircases page.
  • Skirting, architrave, and mouldings — finished to match or complement the existing house detailing
  • Internal doors — supply and hanging of internal doors, including matching to existing door styles in the house
  • Bespoke storage and fitted furniture — making the most of new space with purpose-built joinery. See our bespoke joinery page.
  • Utility rooms and boot rooms — common in rear extensions. See our utility rooms page.

Working with your build team

We are used to working as part of a larger project team — coordinating with builders on programme, working around plasterers and decorators, and taking direction from architects and project managers. If you are a homeowner managing your own extension project and need a reliable joinery contractor who will show up, communicate clearly, and deliver good work, that is exactly what we provide.

York's permitted development rules and conservation area restrictions mean extension projects in the city often require careful planning. We are experienced in working within these constraints and advise on what is and is not possible at an early stage.

Working with us on your extension
  • We coordinate with your builder on programme and access
  • Skirting and architrave matched to your existing house where possible
  • Kitchen fitting timed to follow plastering and before decorating
  • All joinery completed to a consistent standard throughout
  • Clean, tidy working — we respect the build environment

Frequently asked questions

Do you handle first fix as well as second fix carpentry?
Yes — we can be involved from the structural timber stage through to the finished joinery details, or we can come in at second fix stage once plastering is complete. We agree the scope at the quoting stage.
Can you match the existing joinery in my house?
In most cases yes. We can match skirting profiles, architrave styles, and door designs so the extension feels consistent with the existing house rather than visibly different.
Do you fit kitchens in open-plan extensions?
Yes — open-plan kitchen extensions are one of the most common projects we work on in York. The kitchen fitting is typically scheduled after plastering and before decorating. See our kitchen installation page for full detail.
Can you work directly with my architect or builder?
Yes — we are used to coordinating directly with architects, builders, and project managers. Clear communication and reliable programme management are things we take seriously.
Do you cover conservation area extensions in York?
Yes. Many York properties are in conservation areas or are listed, and extension projects here have specific requirements. We have experience working within these constraints and can advise on what is feasible at an early stage.
