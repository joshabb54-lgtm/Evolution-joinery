Home/Services/Utility Rooms

A utility room should be one of the hardest-working spaces in your home. Done well, it takes pressure off the kitchen, gives you somewhere to deal with laundry, muddy boots, and cleaning supplies, and keeps the main living spaces feeling clean and ordered. Done badly, it is a cluttered afterthought that never quite works.

We fit utility rooms across York and the surrounding area — from a single run of base units and a bespoke storage cupboard to full utility room installations with washing machines, tumble dryers, Belfast sinks, and bespoke overhead storage.

What a utility room installation covers

  • Base unit installation — Base cabinets for washing machines, dryers, and storage, levelled and fitted to the space.
  • Wall unit and overhead storage — Additional storage above appliances and over worktop runs, making use of available wall space.
  • Worktop fitting — Laminate, solid wood, or other surfaces cut and fitted over base units. See our worktop fitting page for materials detail.
  • Belfast sink and butler sink housing — Freestanding-style sinks built into a housing unit or supported on a bespoke frame, with correct worktop cutout and edging.
  • Plumbing cupboard — Boiler, hot water cylinder, or water softener housed neatly in a fitted cupboard with correct clearances and access panels.
  • Appliance integration — Under-counter washing machines and dryers fitted under worktop with correct ventilation. See our appliance integration page.
  • Bespoke storage — Coat hooks, shoe storage, dog washing station, built-in ironing board housing — utility rooms often benefit from purpose-built elements that standard cabinets cannot provide.

Matching your kitchen or standalone

If your utility room is adjacent to or visible from the kitchen, it makes sense for the cabinetry to complement the kitchen style — matching door fronts, handles, and worktop material create a cohesive look. We can fit utility rooms to match existing kitchens we have fitted or to match kitchens fitted by others.

Utility rooms do not need to be plain and functional either. Some of the best utility rooms we have fitted have a character of their own — painted shaker doors, oak worktops, and metro tile splashbacks. It is worth considering what you want from the space before settling on the spec.

Utility room planning checklist
  • Decide where the washing machine and dryer will sit — stacked or side by side
  • Consider sink position relative to plumbing routes
  • Plan overhead storage height around appliance doors
  • Think about ventilation for dryer if not heat pump type
  • Allow for boiler access if it sits in the utility room
  • Consider a dog wash or boot storage area if relevant

Frequently asked questions

Can you fit a utility room to match my existing kitchen?
Yes — we can match door styles, finishes, and worktop materials to an existing kitchen whether we fitted it or not. Bring photos and dimensions of your current kitchen when we visit to quote.
Do you fit Belfast sinks?
Yes. Belfast and butler sinks are a popular choice for utility rooms. We build the housing unit and fit the worktop around the sink. Plumbing connections are coordinated with a qualified plumber.
Can you fit a utility room in a small space?
Absolutely. Some of our best utility room fits have been in compact spaces — a single wall, a former pantry, or a narrow corridor. Bespoke cabinetry often makes more sense than standard units in tight spaces.
How long does a utility room installation take?
Most utility room installations take one to three days depending on complexity. A simple run with an appliance or two is usually a single day job.
