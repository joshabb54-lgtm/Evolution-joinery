Home/Services/Bespoke Joinery

Bespoke joinery is the craft of building things specifically for the space they will live in. Not catalogue sizes, not off-the-shelf products with filler panels to cover the gaps — pieces made to fit your walls, your ceilings, your alcoves, and your life.

At Evolution Joinery we are based in Fulford, York, and we design and build bespoke joinery for homes across the city and surrounding area. Whether you need a single run of fitted shelving in an awkward alcove or a complete fitted interior across several rooms, we approach each project with the same level of care.

What bespoke joinery covers

  • Alcove units and shelving — built into chimney breast recesses, under-stair spaces, and awkward wall configurations
  • Fitted wardrobes and bedroom furniture — floor-to-ceiling storage designed around your room
  • Home office joinery — fitted desks, shelving, and storage built into available space
  • Bookcases and media units — freestanding or fitted, built to your exact dimensions
  • Window seats and storage benches — maximising bay windows and alcove spaces
  • Boot rooms and hallway storage — fitted coat storage, shoe racks, and hooks built cleanly
  • Kitchen cabinetry — see our dedicated bespoke cabinetry page

Why bespoke beats off the shelf

York has a high proportion of period properties — Victorian terraces, Georgian townhouses, Edwardian semis — where standard furniture simply does not fit well. Ceiling heights vary. Alcoves are rarely symmetrical. Floors are rarely level. These are exactly the conditions where bespoke joinery delivers results that flat-pack furniture never can.

A set of alcove shelves built to your exact chimney breast, painted to match your walls, with a consistent reveal and well-proportioned detailing looks like it was always part of the house. A set of standard shelves propped in the same space never does.

What to expect from our bespoke joinery service
  • We measure the actual space — nothing is assumed from plans or photos
  • We advise on materials, finishes, and detailing before you commit
  • All joinery is built to your specific dimensions, not standard sizes
  • We work cleanly in your home and protect surrounding surfaces
  • Paint-ready or finished, depending on your preference

Frequently asked questions

What materials do you use for bespoke joinery?
We work in MDF, hardwood, softwood, veneered board, and combinations depending on the application. MDF is ideal for painted finishes. Hardwood and veneered board work better where a natural grain finish is wanted. We advise on the best choice for each project.
Can you match existing joinery in my home?
Yes — matching period moulding profiles, architrave styles, and paint finishes is something we do regularly, particularly in York's older properties where maintaining consistency matters.
Do you paint the finished joinery?
We can deliver joinery paint-ready (primed and ready for your decorator) or fully finished in a colour of your choice. We will confirm this at the quoting stage.
How long does bespoke joinery take?
It depends on the scope. A single alcove unit typically takes one to two days on site. A larger project across multiple rooms will be planned and phased accordingly. We give realistic timelines at the quoting stage.
Do you work in period properties in York?
Yes — this is where bespoke joinery is most valuable. Victorian and Edwardian properties have character that standardised furniture cannot complement well. We understand the conventions of period interiors and build accordingly.
