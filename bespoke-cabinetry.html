Standard kitchen units are designed to fit most kitchens — which means they rarely fit any kitchen perfectly. Awkward corners, sloped ceilings, chimney breasts, alcoves, and non-standard wall lengths all create spaces that off-the-shelf cabinets simply cannot fill well without ugly filler panels or wasted space.

Bespoke cabinetry is the answer. Built specifically for your kitchen, every cabinet is made to the exact dimensions your space requires. The result is a kitchen that looks considered, uses every inch intelligently, and feels like it was always meant to be there.

At Evolution Joinery, we design and build bespoke kitchen cabinetry for homes across York and the surrounding area. Whether you need a single run of base units in an awkward recess or a complete bespoke kitchen from scratch, we work from measurements of your actual space rather than catalogue dimensions.

When bespoke cabinetry makes sense

Not every kitchen needs bespoke cabinets. Standard units work well in regular, rectangular spaces. But if your kitchen has any of the following, bespoke is often the better route:

  • Irregular dimensions — walls that are not square, or room lengths that do not correspond to standard unit widths
  • Sloped or low ceilings — common in older York properties and extensions where wall unit height needs to vary
  • Chimney breasts or alcoves — spaces that need cabinets built into and around them cleanly
  • Pantry or larder units — tall bespoke storage built to use ceiling height fully
  • Island units — freestanding or built-in islands built to the exact size and height required
  • Wraparound or L-shaped layouts — where corners need special treatment

What we build

Our bespoke cabinetry service covers base units, wall units, tall units, islands, and larder cabinets. Frequently asked questions

How much more expensive is bespoke cabinetry than standard units?
It depends heavily on materials and complexity. Bespoke is generally more expensive than flat-pack but the gap narrows with higher-end standard ranges. The right comparison is total cost including filler panels, workarounds, and wasted space — bespoke often delivers better value per square metre of usable storage.
Can you match existing cabinetry for an extension or additional run?
Yes, in many cases. We can match door styles, profiles, and finishes to extend an existing kitchen or add cabinetry to a new space without it looking mismatched.
What materials do you use?
We work in solid hardwood, painted MDF, veneered boards, and combinations of these depending on the look and budget. We will recommend what makes sense for your specific project.
How long does bespoke cabinetry take to make and fit?
Lead time from survey to installation varies by project complexity, typically two to six weeks. A full bespoke kitchen will take longer than a single run of cabinets. We will give you a realistic timeline when we quote.
Do you work on kitchens in period York properties?
Yes — this is actually where bespoke cabinetry excels. Older properties with non-standard dimensions, uneven floors, and sloped ceilings are exactly the kind of spaces bespoke cabinetry is designed for.
