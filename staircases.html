Home/Services/Staircases

A staircase is one of the most visible pieces of joinery in any home. It sets the tone for the space around it and, in many York properties, is one of the first things you see when you walk through the front door. When it is well made and well fitted it adds real character. When it is tired, damaged, or poorly specified it drags down the whole interior.

We build and fit staircases across York and the surrounding area — from full replacements in period properties to purpose-built stairs for loft conversions and extensions. Every staircase is built to the specific dimensions of the opening, to building regulations, and to a standard that will last for decades.

Staircase work we carry out

  • Full staircase replacement — remove the existing staircase and install a new one built to the exact opening dimensions, compliant with current regulations
  • Loft conversion staircases — space-efficient staircase designs for loft conversions, making the best use of available floor space on both levels
  • Extension staircases — new stairs for multi-level extensions, designed to work with the existing or new layout
  • Spindle and balustrade replacement — replacing outdated or damaged spindles with new turned, square, or metal spindles; new handrails and newel posts
  • Staircase refurbishment — repairing or replacing individual treads and risers, tightening loose elements, and bringing an existing staircase back to a solid, safe standard
  • Open tread and contemporary designs — open riser staircases and contemporary designs for modern extensions and new builds

Staircases in period York properties

Many of York's Victorian and Edwardian terraces have original staircases that are structurally sound but cosmetically tired — worn treads, loose spindles, dated handrails. In these cases a refurbishment or selective replacement can restore the staircase to a standard that respects the period character of the house without requiring a full replacement.

Where a full replacement is needed, we can build a new staircase that references the period detailing of the property — turned spindles, square newel posts, moulded handrails — so the new staircase feels like it belongs rather than contrasting with the surrounding interior.

Frequently asked questions

Do new staircases need to comply with building regulations?
Yes — any new staircase or significant alteration must comply with Part K of the Building Regulations, covering dimensions, handrail heights, and balustrade spacing. We build all staircases to the correct specification.
Can you replace just the spindles and handrail without replacing the whole staircase?
Yes. Spindle and balustrade replacement is a common job that transforms the look of a staircase without the cost of a full replacement. We can replace turned spindles with square section, add metal balusters, or fit a new handrail profile while leaving the treads and structure in place.
How long does a staircase replacement take?
A straightforward staircase replacement in a standard terraced or semi-detached York property typically takes two to three days. More complex designs or larger properties will take longer. We will give you a realistic timeline when we quote.
Can you build a staircase for a loft conversion?
Yes — loft conversion staircases are a significant part of our work. The challenge is usually making the staircase space-efficient without compromising on usability or regulations. We have built loft stairs in a wide range of configurations across York. See our loft conversions page.
What materials do you use?
Most staircases are built in softwood (pine) for painted finishes or oak and hardwood for stained or natural finishes. We advise on the best choice for your project and budget at the quoting stage.
