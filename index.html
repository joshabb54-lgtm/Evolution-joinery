Evolution Joinery is a York-based kitchen fitting business operating out of Fulford. We bring a craftsman's approach to every kitchen project, combining precision joinery skills with an eye for the details that make a real difference to how a kitchen looks and functions.

Whether you've bought flat-pack units or are investing in a full bespoke kitchen, we treat every install with the same care and professionalism. Honest pricing, reliable timekeeping, and a finish you can be proud of.

What we do

From unit installation to the finishing details, we handle every aspect of your kitchen renovation to a high standard.

Full Kitchen Installation

Complete kitchen fitting from start to finish. We handle unit assembly, cabinet installation, plinths, cornices, and every detail in between.

Bespoke Cabinetry

Handbuilt kitchen cabinetry made precisely to your space. No standard sizing, no off-the-shelf compromises. Built to last and fitted to perfection.

Worktop Fitting

Laminate, solid wood, or stone worktops cut, jointed, and fitted cleanly. Precise templating and tight seams as standard.

Appliance Integration

Ovens, hobs, dishwashers, and integrated fridge-freezers fitted and built in flush to the cabinetry. Neat, seamless, and functional.

Utility Rooms

Utility room fitting to match your kitchen or as a standalone project. Built-in storage, plumbing cupboards, and laundry units fitted properly.

Kitchen Renovations

Refreshing or replacing an existing kitchen. We can reuse carcasses, reface doors, or take the whole lot out and start fresh. You decide the scope.
