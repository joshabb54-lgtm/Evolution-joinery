Loft Conversions — York

Loft conversions in York.

Transform unused roof space into beautiful, functional living space. We handle the full internal fit-out — from the staircase to the final fitted detail.

Home/Services/Loft Conversions

A loft conversion is one of the most effective ways to add living space and value to a York property. Done well, it creates a room that feels intentional and integrated with the rest of the home. Done badly, it feels like an afterthought — low-quality finishes, a staircase that eats up space, and storage that was never properly thought through.

At Evolution Joinery we handle the joinery and internal fit-out side of loft conversions across York and the surrounding area. We work alongside structural engineers, builders, and architects on full projects, or we can take on the fit-out stage of a conversion that is structurally complete.

What we handle in a loft conversion

  • Staircase design and fitting — space-efficient staircase solutions built and fitted to the conversion. See our staircases page for detail.
  • Dormer window joinery — internal lining, reveals, and finishing around new dormer openings
  • Velux and rooflight surrounds — clean, well-finished openings that make the most of the natural light
  • Eaves storage — fitted storage built into the low-height eaves areas, making use of space that would otherwise be wasted
  • Fitted wardrobes and bedroom furniture — bespoke furniture built to the exact dimensions of the conversion space, including sloped ceiling sections
  • Internal wall lining and boarding — lining out the structural elements to create finished, habitable surfaces
  • Doors and door frames — internal door fitting, including fire doors where required

Loft conversions in York properties

York's housing stock is well-suited to loft conversion. The Victorian and Edwardian terraces that make up much of the city's residential areas typically have pitched roofs with enough headroom for a usable loft space, and the addition of a dormer or rear extension of the roof plane can create substantial rooms.

The character of the property matters. A sympathetically converted loft in a period York terrace should feel like a natural continuation of the house — not a bolt-on. The joinery detailing, the choice of materials, and the way natural light is managed all contribute to this. We work with these considerations in mind on every conversion we fit out.

Loft conversion fit-out — what good looks like
  • Staircase positioned to minimise loss of space on the floor below
  • Eaves storage integrated into the build, not added as an afterthought
  • Natural light from dormers and Velux windows maximised by clean reveals
  • Sloped ceilings used as a design feature, not a problem to work around
  • All joinery finished to a consistent standard throughout the space

Frequently asked questions

Do you handle the full loft conversion or just the fit-out?
We specialise in the joinery and internal fit-out stage. We work as part of a wider team on full conversion projects or can take over from a builder once the structural work and plastering is complete. We are happy to coordinate with your builder or recommend contractors we have worked with.
Can you fit a staircase into a tight space?
Yes — space efficiency is one of the most important considerations for loft staircase design. We build and fit staircases that make the best use of available space without compromising comfort or building regulations. See our staircases page for more detail.
Can you fit wardrobes and storage into sloped ceiling areas?
Absolutely — bespoke fitted storage that follows the line of a sloped ceiling is one of our most common loft conversion jobs. It makes use of space that standard furniture cannot fill and gives the room a much more considered, finished feel.
Do you work in conservation areas or listed buildings in York?
Yes, with appropriate care. Loft conversions in conservation areas or listed buildings in York have specific planning and consent requirements. We are experienced in working within these constraints and advise accordingly.
